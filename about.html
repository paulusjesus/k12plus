Why we exist

The maths crisis is not the learners' fault

In Namibia, 84% of Grade 12 learners fail mathematics and only 15.3% qualify for university. In one exam cycle, 420 learners scored zero. These are not lazy children. They are learners without the personalised support that meaningful learning requires.

Meanwhile teachers spend 60 to 80% of their time on administration instead of teaching. k12plus exists to break that cycle: personal AI tutoring for every learner, and automation that gives teachers their time back.
