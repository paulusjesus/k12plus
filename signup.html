Privacy Policy

K-Twelve Educational Technologies CC · Effective 5 August 2025

The short version: your data exists to power your learning - nothing else. We never sell it, we never market to children, and parents stay in control. Every quiz you take and question you ask makes the tutor better at teaching you.

1. Introduction

At K-Twelve Educational Technologies CC ("we", "our" or "us"), privacy is not a legal afterthought - it is the foundation of a platform trusted by learners, parents and schools across Namibia and South Africa. This policy explains, in plain language, how we collect, use, protect and share personal information across the k12plus platform: the AI tutor, USSD access, learner, parent and educator dashboards, and related services. By using k12plus, you agree to these terms.

2. What information we collect

We collect only what is needed to personalise learning and improve the platform:

  • Personal information - name, age, grade and class, school, location (region only, never GPS), and contact details of parents, guardians or educators.
  • Educational data - quiz results, learning patterns, engagement history and performance, used to adapt the tutor to each learner.
  • Device and technical data - device type, browser, and access method (web, mobile or USSD), so k12plus works on any phone.
  • Voice and handwriting input - collected only where applicable, to power tutoring and feedback.

3. How we use your information

  • Personalise and adapt learning content to each learner's pace and curriculum
  • Track academic progress and celebrate growth
  • Provide real-time dashboards for teachers and parents
  • Support troubleshooting and respond to user feedback
  • Generate anonymous, aggregated analytics that make the platform better for everyone

4. Consent

We explicitly request consent before collecting or processing personal data - especially for minors. For users under 18, parental or institutional consent is required, in line with Namibian law and global data-protection standards. Consent can be withdrawn at any time.

5. Do we sell your data?

No. Never. We do not sell, rent or trade your personal or non-personal information - not to advertisers, not to data brokers, not to anyone. Your data is used strictly to support your educational experience.

6. Data security

Your data is protected by:

  • Encrypted cloud storage, in transit and at rest
  • Strict, role-based access controls
  • Regular security updates and independent audits

7. Children's data & marketing

Children's data is never used for marketing - not by k12plus, and not by any third party. Every data point we hold about a learner exists for one purpose only: their education.

8. Third-party services

We use a small number of carefully vetted third-party tools - cloud hosting (e.g. Microsoft Azure), analytics and support services. Each is assessed for child-data protection and GDPR compliance before it touches the platform.

9. Access, correction & deletion

Students, parents and school administrators can request access to their personal data, correction of inaccuracies, or deletion of records at any time. Email paulus@k12edu.io and we will act on it promptly.

10. Data retention

We keep data only as long as necessary to deliver our services, meet legal obligations and support educational reporting. Once it is no longer needed, it is securely deleted or anonymised.

11. Policy updates

As our practices evolve, this policy will too. Updates are posted at www.k12plus.app/privacy-policy, and significant changes are announced by email or in-platform message.
